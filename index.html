03 / Background

Engineering beyond the surface.

I’m pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering with a specialization in Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics. I’m building a foundation in application security, systems analysis, and software development.

I’m most interested in work that combines investigation with implementation: tracing a request through a system, identifying where trust is assumed, testing those assumptions, and documenting what the evidence shows.

This portfolio is a living record of that process. I aim to show not only what I build, but also how I reason about design decisions, limitations, and security trade-offs.
